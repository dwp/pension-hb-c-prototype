Apply for Pension Credit and Housing Benefit

If you have been invited by DWP, you can use this service to apply for both Pension Credit and Housing Benefit at the same time.

Pension Credit can top up your income to help with living costs, and Housing Benefit can help you pay your rent.

Applying for Pension Credit

You can use this service to apply for Pension Credit if you:

  • are currently in the UK
  • have the right to live and work in the UK

You cannot use this service to apply for Pension Credit if you:

  • are currently outside the UK
  • have been outside the UK for more than 4 weeks, unless it is for medical treatment or recovery
  • are moving away from the UK permanently
  • are below State Pension age

Applying for Housing Benefit

You can use this service to apply for Housing Benefit if you:

  • are resident in Great Britain
  • currently live at the address you are applying for
  • you are responsible for the rent for the home you live in, or the room you live in

You cannot use this service to apply for Housing Benefit if you:

  • are currently staying away from the address you are applying for
  • own your home, or have a mortgage on it

Before you apply

You can use this service to check if you are eligible for both benefits before applying. You may only be eligible for one, or neither.

You will need to create a GOV.UK One Login account as part of your application.

You must complete your application within 30 days or you will have to start again.

Continuing an application you have saved

You can save your answers and return to complete your application, but you must complete it within 30 days or you will have to start again.

Sign in to continue your application

Other help you may be entitled to

If you are eligible for Pension Credit you can also get other help, such as:

  • Cold Weather Payments (or Winter Heating Payments in Scotland)
  • a Council Tax Reduction
  • a free TV licence if you’re aged 75 or over
  • help with NHS dental treatment, glasses and transport costs for hospital appointments, if you get the Guarantee Credit part of Pension Credit
  • help with your heating costs through the Warm Home Discount Scheme

Information you will need

You will need to tell us about:

  • your right to live and work in the UK
  • any time spent outside the UK in the last 3 months
  • any overnight stays in hospital in the last 3 months
  • your living situation, including any landlord or agency details
  • any other people living with you
  • your address history for the last 3 months
  • your income, money and investments
  • any property or land you own

If you live with a partner, you will also need to tell us the same information about them.
