About your Pension Credit application

We will contact you by letter with our decision about your Pension Credit application.

If we need more information, we may contact you by letter, phone or SMS.

If your application is successful, your Pension Credit will be paid into the account you gave us during your application.

You will be able to change this to a different account once your Pension Credit is in payment.

About your Housing Benefit application

Your local authority will contact you by letter with their decision about your Housing Benefit application.

If they need more information, they may contact you by letter, telephone or email.

If your application is successful, your Housing Benefit will be paid into the account you told us about during your application.

You may be able to change this to a different account once your Housing Benefit is in payment.

Contacting you about your application

If you need help with your application, contact the Pension Credit and Housing Benefit helpline on 0800 720 148.

You will need to tell us your National Insurance number when you call.

Other benefits and financial support you may be able to get

Council Tax Reduction

You told us that you want to claim Council Tax Reduction as part of your application. Your local authority will contact you about this separately.

Carer's Allowance

You might be able to claim Carer's Allowance if you care for someone for 35 hours a week or more.
